# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; hosted on Cloudflare Pages (per the existing project README).

## Users

Visitors who want to discover and understand Yuzuctus's projects, open the tools, or find the owner's profiles.

## Product Purpose

Yuzuhub is Yuzuctus's public home page for their projects and profiles. Success means visitors can quickly understand what each real project does and reach it or a relevant profile.

## Positioning

The hub gathers independent tools and experiments made around Yuzuctus's own needs and interests; it is not a storefront or a product platform.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Links to projects and social/game profiles.
- French and English language controls.
- Light and dark theme controls.
- Selected music and an in-development Yuzuctus.fm project.
- Keep public copy factual and useful; do not invent features, status, audience metrics, or commercial claims.
- No public search, filters, or project tags.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the Yuzuctus name and the user's OC art, with the existing artist credits intact.
- Retain the established mint green `#A6E9D1` and use Nunito, the font named in the repository's previous tokens (the user called it “Numito”).
- Keep interface copy professional, concise, and factual even when the illustrations or visual tone are playful. Avoid cute slogans and sentimental filler.

## Evidence on Hand

- OC image assets and artist credits are present in `img/Characters/` and the project markup.
- A real Osurea interface capture is available at `img/osurea-preview.png`.
- Project links and factual descriptions are in `index.html`; the GitHub profile and linked project repositories provide additional evidence.
- The user has not supplied a ZestEcho image for the hub.

## Product Principles

- Help visitors find a project or profile without unnecessary scrolling or interface chrome.
- Describe what a project does in plain, verifiable language.
- Treat the OC art and its creators as authored work and credit them.
- Keep each project independent; the hub links to projects rather than implying one unified product.
